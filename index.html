ABOUT THE TOURNAMENT

Ire-Akari Unity Cup

The Ire-Akari Unity Cup is a community-driven football tournament designed to promote unity, teamwork, healthy competition and talent development.

It provides a platform for teams and players to showcase their skills, build friendships and create lasting memories through football.
