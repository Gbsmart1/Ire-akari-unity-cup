ABOUT THE TOURNAMENT

More than a football competition.

Ire-Akari Unity Cup is designed to create an exciting platform for local football talent while strengthening friendship, community spirit and healthy competition.

Our goal is to make every match an opportunity for players and supporters to connect, compete fairly and celebrate the community.

OUR VISION

One Community.
One Game.
One Unity.

Building stronger relationships through sport.
